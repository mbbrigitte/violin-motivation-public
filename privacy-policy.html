Privacy Policy — Violin Motivation Adventure

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Violin Motivation Adventure is a children's violin practice and motivation app developed by Brigitte Mueller.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Violin Motivation Adventure does not collect personal information.

The app does not require users to create an account or provide a name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, photograph, or other personal information.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party services that collect user information.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store information locally on your device, such as practice progress, game progress, and app settings.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

The app does not transmit personal information to the developer or to third-party services.

Children's Privacy

Violin Motivation Adventure is designed for children and families.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require children to provide personal information in order to use its features.

External Website

The app may provide a link to the developer's website, Violin Motivation Adventure, from a parent-oriented section of the app.

The website may contain information about the app and may provide additional products or services in the future. This Privacy Policy applies to the app itself. The website may have its own privacy practices and policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
